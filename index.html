This tool is used to check the distance of houses from the quarry area as part of a preliminary assessment of blasting-related damage to structures around the quarry blasting area. Distances and screening zones support field assessment and do not, on their own, establish the cause of structural damage.

Method and limitations

Quarry boundary: The quarry area was polygonized in EPSG:4326 and transformed to EPSG:32737 for distance measurement. Every distance is the shortest horizontal distance to its polygon boundary; a house inside the quarry polygon has a screening distance of 0 m.

Zones: Zone A — High proximity (0–500 m); Zone B — Moderate proximity (>500–1,000 m); Zone C — Low proximity (>1,000–1,500 m); Zone D — Outside screening zone (>1,500 m). These are proximity screening classes, not predicted blasting influence or evidence of damage.

700 m circle: Independent centre-based buffer around the quarry centre; its edge clearance is measured independently of the actual quarry boundary.

Uncertainty: GPS error and polygon positional accuracy can affect classifications near thresholds. Treat near-boundary measurements as needing confirmation.
